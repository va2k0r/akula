# Strategic weapon-selector visual reference

The runtime weapon drawings are original inline SVG line art. No third-party
image is shipped. Their readable mechanical features were informed by public
patent drawings rather than copied from a single weapon illustration:

- torpedo: elongated cylindrical hull, acoustic nose section, sectional rings,
  aft control surfaces and coaxial propulsion, after US 3,054,371 and US
  2,414,928;
- tube-launched missile: cylindrical launch profile with folding wing and tail
  surfaces, after EP 0,257,163 and US 4,121,606;
- moored mine: spherical case and lower mooring connection, after US 1,471,628.

References:

- https://patents.google.com/patent/US3054371A/en
- https://patents.google.com/patent/US2414928A/en
- https://patents.google.com/patent/EP0257163A1/en
- https://patents.google.com/patent/US4121606A/en
- https://patents.google.com/patent/US1471628/en

The labels are category names, not claims that a specific historical loadout is
already simulated: `ТОРПЕДЫ`, `РАКЕТЫ`, and `МИНЫ`. Only torpedo planning and
salvo release are active in this increment.
